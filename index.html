Update Log

v0.5.3 - Game Speed

  • New 1x, 2x, and 3x speed buttons in the top-left corner of the battlefield (or press 1, 2, or 3 on a keyboard).
  • Faster speed only makes time pass quicker. Towers, spiders, cash, and waves all work exactly the same, just sooner.
  • Pause still works at any speed, and your speed stays when you restart a mission.

v0.5.2 - Volume and Safe Restart

  • New Sound Volume slider from 0% to 100%. Your volume is remembered, and the Sound On/Off button still mutes everything.
  • Fixed the Restart Mission button, which did nothing before.
  • Restart Mission now asks "Are you sure you want to restart?" with No and Yes buttons, so you cannot restart by accident. The game pauses while it asks.

v0.5.1 - Sound Effects

  • Added sound effects: the Magic Cat's spell zaps and critical bursts, spell hits, familiar paw swipes, abyss pulses, spiders popping (Cosmic Spinners shatter), placing, upgrading, selling, wave start, wave cleared, core hits, and Core Lost.
  • New Sound: On/Off button (or press M). Your choice is remembered.
  • All sounds are made by the game itself, so nothing extra to download.

v0.5.0 - The Magic Cat Stands Alone

  • Removed the placeholder towers. The Magic Cat is now the only tower, with all three upgrade paths.
  • New detailed 3D models for the Magic Cat, Voidweavers, and Cosmic Spinners, with real shadows, reflections, and richer lighting.
  • Upgrades now show on the cat: shadow orbs, curse runes, and a spirit familiar.
  • Spell bolts fly to their targets, spiders flash when hit and burst apart when destroyed, and cash pops up on every kill.
  • New space backdrop, a glowing core reactor that reacts when hit, and a swirling spawn portal.
  • Camera controls: drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, plus a Reset View button.
  • Fixed the game running in slow motion on slower devices.
  • Phones: tap once to preview a spot, tap again to place.
  • Losing the core now shows a Core Lost screen instead of restarting instantly.

v0.4.0 - 3D Battlefield

  • The battlefield is now fully 3D with a tilted camera, glowing lanes, lighting, and a starfield.
  • Towers, the Magic Cat, Voidweavers, and Cosmic Spinners are now 3D models. Towers turn to face their targets and spiders walk with animated legs.
  • Beams, spell bolts, paw strikes, and pulse rings now glow in 3D.
  • Gameplay, stats, waves, and controls are unchanged. If 3D cannot load on a device, the game falls back to the classic 2D view.

v0.3.2 - Cosmic Spinner

  • Added Cosmic Spinner as a new blue mechanical spider enemy.
  • Cosmic Spinner starts spawning on wave 5 mixed into the Voidweaver roster.
  • It has more HP, moves a bit slower, rewards more cash, and deals more core damage.
  • Updated the enemy database and wave logs to show mixed waves.

v0.3.1 - Magic Cat Balance

  • Retuned Magic Cat after testing path damage estimates.
  • Shadow remains the strongest raw path, but now sits below Summoning plus Curse combined.
  • Summoning is second through familiars and abyss pulses.
  • Curse is third for direct damage, but has slow, mark, chain, and damage-over-time utility.

v0.3.0 - Magic Cat

  • Added Magic Cat with three upgrade paths, spell bolts, paw effects, curse effects, and tower inspection upgrades.
