Mission Notes

Cosmic Spinner joins on wave 5. Star Mender healers join on wave 10: kill them first. Nebula Brood joins on wave 15 and bursts into 3 fast Broodlings when destroyed. The boss Arachnos attacks every 10th wave. After wave 25 the swarm gets thicker, tougher and harder to stun every wave.

Update Log

v0.13.0 - Arachnos

  • New boss: Arachnos, a giant armored silver spider with glowing red eyes. He attacks every 10th wave (10, 20, 30...) and gets stronger each time, with his own health bar at the top of the screen.
  • Armor Plates: his health bar has 3 parts. He takes 40% less damage until 2 plates are broken, then his core is exposed and he takes full damage but moves faster. Every broken plate staggers him and calls in more spiders.
  • Unstoppable: Arachnos can't be pushed back, stuns and slows barely work on him, and healers can't heal him. Davy Jones' Locker takes 10% of his health instead of grabbing him.
  • Defeat him for a big cash reward. If he reaches the core, you lose 35% core.
  • Endgame fix (after wave 25): spiders keep coming faster and in packs, get tougher every wave, and shake off stuns, slows and pushbacks more and more. Cash per spider and wave bonuses grow slower.
  • New Damage Meter button: see what share of all damage each tower type does (adds up to 100%) and how much. Tap a placed tower to see its own damage dealt.
  • Removed the small yellow cash numbers when spiders die, for less lag. You still get the cash.

v0.12.2 - Bug Fixes

  • Galactic Squirrel (Nut Hoard): star acorns from each wave are now auto-collected when that wave is cleared, even while Auto Wave keeps the next waves coming. Acorns that had not dropped yet are paid too.
  • Ghost Ship (Set Sail): the ship now sails out to the path and cruises up and down a long stretch of it, instead of barely moving.
  • Tide Warden: fixed the 3 second freeze when buying Poseidon's Judgment.

v0.12.1 - Spider HP Curve

  • Early waves are softer: spiders start with about 24% less HP on wave 1.
  • Spider HP now ramps up faster every wave. It matches the old numbers around wave 10, then gets tougher: about 27% more HP by wave 20 and 58% more by wave 30.
  • Applies to every spider type.

v0.12.0 - Tide Warden

  • New tower: the Tide Warden ($400), a fish warrior with a glowing blue trident. He calls a trident down from the sky that spikes into the deck and splashes every spider around it.
  • Spiders hit by the splash get Soaked: 15% slower, and every tower hits them 15% harder. Lightning does double damage to Soaked spiders.
  • Water units: the Tide Warden and the Ghost Ship can stand on water. In water the Tide Warden strikes 25% faster and the Ghost Ship gets +20% range.
  • Storm Trident path: Heavier Trident, Wider Impact, Lightning Strike, Twin Strike, and Poseidon's Judgment, a colossal trident every 15s.
  • Tide Caller path: Splash Puddles, Bigger Puddles, Whirlpool, Riptide, and the Tsunami that crashes down the whole path once a wave.
  • Abyss Lord path: Bubble Trap, Bubble Barrage, Piranha School, Pressure of the Deep (all nearby allies hit 1 extra spider), and Kraken Rising.
  • Auto Wave now works like Bloons Apopalypse: the next wave starts as soon as the last spider of the current wave leaves the portal, even if spiders are still on the map. Each wave pays its bonus once all of its own spiders are gone. With Auto Wave off, nothing changed.
  • Attack effects glow-up: glowing crescent slashes and petals for the Rabbit Ronin, plus shadow copies during Moon Dash. Spinning spell orbs, rune circles, star crits, curse marks and a ghostly claw for the Magic Cat. Starry acorn trails and confetti bursts for the Squirrel. Gunport flashes, smoke and ghost-fire trails for the Ghost Ship.
  • Hits leave fading marks on the deck, and tier 5 powers light up the screen with a flash.
  • Tower damage, prices and upgrades for the other units are unchanged.

v0.11.0 - Maps

  • New map menu when the game loads. Each map shows a preview, a star rating (more stars = harder), a difficulty label and your best wave on it.
  • New Maps button that asks before taking you back to the menu.
  • Station Deck (1 star, Beginner): the map you know, unchanged.
  • Neptune Rig (3 stars, Intermediate): a rig on stilts over an alien ocean with a stormy sky. A shorter path crosses two bridges. Only the Ghost Ship can be placed on water, and it gets +20% range there. Spiders wading through shallow water on the walkway slow down by 15%. Rain falls during heavy waves.
  • Twin Gates (4 stars, Advanced): the dark lower deck. Two portals take turns sending spiders, and both paths meet right before the core.
  • Towers, upgrades, spiders, waves and cash are unchanged.

v0.10.0 - Visual Makeover

  • The path is now a raised metal walkway with glowing edge lights that run toward the core and pulsing direction arrows.
  • New station floor: deck plates, vents, hatches, scuffs and blinking lights. Every tower now sits on a glowing build pad, and a hex grid shows while you place one.
  • Moodier lighting with a swirling portal that lights the floor. Heavy waves (every 5th wave from wave 10) turn on red alarm lights and a HEAVY WAVE warning.
  • Spiders flash and wobble when hit, burst into glowing pieces when they die, and have glowing eyes, colored underglow and cleaner health bars.
  • Hit Numbers button shows or hides damage numbers.
  • New sci-fi menus with pictures of each unit, plus tier bars on upgrades.
  • Quality button (High / Low) for phones. Phones that run slowly switch to Low on their own.
  • No gameplay changes.

v0.9.0 - Rabbit Ronin

  • New starter tower: the Rabbit Ronin ($80), the cheapest unit in the game. He slashes with his katana every 0.3s, about twice as fast as the Magic Cat, but has a short reach, so place him right next to the path.
  • Way of the Blade path: sharper steel, Twin Katanas, Armor Cutter for big spiders, Wind Slash, and Thousand Cuts.
  • Shuriken path: throwing stars for more range, Double Throw, Poison Stars, Ricochet, and the Shuriken Storm ring.
  • Shadow Dash path: Swift Paws, Shadow Step, Hit and Run, Battle Cry (all nearby allies attack faster), and Moon Dash along the whole path.
  • Same upgrade rules as the other towers: 3 paths, 5 tiers each, only one path past tier 2.
  • New 3D Rabbit Ronin with a red sun behind him, upgrade looks for every tier, and new sounds.

v0.8.0 - Ghost Ship

  • New tower: the Ghost Ship ($1,000). A cursed ship that floats over the station and fires cannonballs from both sides, with a huge range.
  • Only 3 Ghost Ships can be placed. Try a 4th and you'll get a "Max 3 Ghost Ships placed" message.
  • Cursed Cannons path: heavier shot, more gunports, Chain Shot, green Hellfire Rounds, and the Ghost Armada that hits every spider on the map.
  • Phantom Crew path: a Lookout for more range, ghost pirates on the path, spiders that rise again to fight for you, and Davy Jones' Locker, a giant hand that drags the strongest spider under.
  • Flying Dutchman path: slowing fog, a Haunting Bell, a ship that sails out over the path, a Ghostly Ram, and the Voyage of the Damned that sails the whole path backward.
  • Same upgrade rules as the other towers: 3 paths, 5 tiers each, only one path past tier 2.
  • New 3D ship with torn glowing sails and mist, upgrade looks for every tier, and new sounds.

v0.7.0 - Galactic Squirrel

  • New tower: the Galactic Squirrel ($150). It throws glowing star acorns that burst on groups of spiders.
  • Star Acorns path: more damage, bigger bursts, Cracked Shell (hit spiders can't be healed for 3s), Cluster Acorns, and the Supernova Nut that stuns.
  • Nut Hoard path: earns cash every wave, drops star acorns you tap to collect (any left are gathered when the wave ends), speeds up nearby towers, and the Galactic Hoard pays about $600 a wave.
  • Galaxy Tail path: slowing acorns, a tail swipe that knocks spiders back, a galaxy patch on the path that slows them, a Gravity Well that pulls them back, and the Milky Way Whirl that sends spiders far back down the path.
  • Same upgrade rules as the Magic Cat: 3 paths, 5 tiers each, only one path past tier 2.
  • New 3D model with a glowing galaxy tail, upgrade looks for every tier, and new sounds.

v0.6.2 - Bigger Start

  • You now start each mission with $600 instead of $300, enough for 5 Magic Cats, so wave 1 is winnable.

v0.6.1 - Station Edge

  • The battlefield is now a space-station deck floating in orbit, with a thick metal rim, panel seams, and a row of lit station windows along the sides.
  • Running lights chase around the rim and flash red when the core is hit.
  • Four corner pylons with blinking beacons, and a radar dish turning on a deck off the back edge.
  • Docking clamps with warning stripes where the spiders enter and where the lane leaves for the core.
  • Glowing power conduits run from the core over the edge and down the hull, turning red when the core takes damage.
  • Cables and pipes hang off the station, a ring of drifting debris and dust circles it, and a colorful nebula glows far below.
  • The far planet now has rings, and a small ship cruises past now and then.
  • Gameplay is unchanged. These details are 3D only.

v0.6.0 - Healers and Broods

  • New enemy: Star Mender (from wave 10). A green healer spider with a glowing star on its back. Every few seconds it sends out a green pulse that heals nearby enemies. Healing does not stack: an enemy can only be healed once per pulse cycle, even with several Menders around.
  • New enemy: Nebula Brood (from wave 15). A slow, tough pink spider carrying three glowing eggs. When it is destroyed it bursts into 3 fast Broodlings.
  • Both have full 3D models and 2D versions, plus new heal and burst sounds.
  • The Enemy Database and wave messages list the new enemies.

v0.5.3 - Game Speed

  • New 1x, 2x, and 3x speed buttons in the top-left corner of the battlefield (or press 1, 2, or 3 on a keyboard).
  • Faster speed only makes time pass quicker. Towers, spiders, cash, and waves all work exactly the same, just sooner.
  • Pause still works at any speed, and your speed stays when you restart a mission.

v0.5.2 - Volume and Safe Restart

  • New Sound Volume slider from 0% to 100%. Your volume is remembered, and the Sound On/Off button still mutes everything.
  • Fixed the Restart Mission button, which did nothing before.
  • Restart Mission now asks "Are you sure you want to restart?" with No and Yes buttons, so you cannot restart by accident. The game pauses while it asks.

v0.5.1 - Sound Effects

  • Added sound effects: the Magic Cat's spell zaps and critical bursts, spell hits, familiar paw swipes, abyss pulses, spiders popping (Cosmic Spinners shatter), placing, upgrading, selling, wave start, wave cleared, core hits, and Core Lost.
  • New Sound: On/Off button (or press M). Your choice is remembered.
  • All sounds are made by the game itself, so nothing extra to download.

v0.5.0 - The Magic Cat Stands Alone

  • Removed the placeholder towers. The Magic Cat is now the only tower, with all three upgrade paths.
  • New detailed 3D models for the Magic Cat, Voidweavers, and Cosmic Spinners, with real shadows, reflections, and richer lighting.
  • Upgrades now show on the cat: shadow orbs, curse runes, and a spirit familiar.
  • Spell bolts fly to their targets, spiders flash when hit and burst apart when destroyed, and cash pops up on every kill.
  • New space backdrop, a glowing core reactor that reacts when hit, and a swirling spawn portal.
  • Camera controls: drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, plus a Reset View button.
  • Fixed the game running in slow motion on slower devices.
  • Phones: tap once to preview a spot, tap again to place.
  • Losing the core now shows a Core Lost screen instead of restarting instantly.

v0.4.0 - 3D Battlefield

  • The battlefield is now fully 3D with a tilted camera, glowing lanes, lighting, and a starfield.
  • Towers, the Magic Cat, Voidweavers, and Cosmic Spinners are now 3D models. Towers turn to face their targets and spiders walk with animated legs.
  • Beams, spell bolts, paw strikes, and pulse rings now glow in 3D.
  • Gameplay, stats, waves, and controls are unchanged. If 3D cannot load on a device, the game falls back to the classic 2D view.

v0.3.2 - Cosmic Spinner

  • Added Cosmic Spinner as a new blue mechanical spider enemy.
  • Cosmic Spinner starts spawning on wave 5 mixed into the Voidweaver roster.
  • It has more HP, moves a bit slower, rewards more cash, and deals more core damage.
  • Updated the enemy database and wave logs to show mixed waves.

v0.3.1 - Magic Cat Balance

  • Retuned Magic Cat after testing path damage estimates.
  • Shadow remains the strongest raw path, but now sits below Summoning plus Curse combined.
  • Summoning is second through familiars and abyss pulses.
  • Curse is third for direct damage, but has slow, mark, chain, and damage-over-time utility.

v0.3.0 - Magic Cat

  • Added Magic Cat with three upgrade paths, spell bolts, paw effects, curse effects, and tower inspection upgrades.
